Per-Farmer Impact Calculator

How IRL Works for Individual Farmers

🎯 The Problem You Identified

Original IRL only showed regional impact (₹124L total loss) but didn't tell farmers: "How much will I lose?"

✅ The Solution

Enhanced IRL now shows per-farmer impact based on:

  • Total regional loss ÷ Number of affected farms
  • Percentage of average annual income (₹2.5L)
  • Individual farmer distress level
  • Personalized recommendations

How the Calculation Works

// Example Calculation
Total Regional Loss: ₹1,24,00,000
Number of Affected Farms: 850
Average Annual Income: ₹2,50,000

Per-Farmer Loss = ₹1,24,00,000 ÷ 850 = ₹1,45,88 per farmer
Loss Percentage = (₹1,45,88 ÷ ₹2,50,000) × 100 = 58%

// Classification:
- <20% = Low Impact (Acceptable)
- 20-50% = Medium Impact (Sensitive) 
- >50% = High Impact (Critical)
